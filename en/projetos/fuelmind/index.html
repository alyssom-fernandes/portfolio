Fuel Mind

Reads fuel invoices (NF-e) and closes the month.

controle-entradas-posto.web.appScreens from the public demo, with fictitious data
Open the demoCode

Why it exists

Fuel deliveries were entered in one spreadsheet per fuel station and TRR (fuel distributor). From those spreadsheets, the data went to another one, which calculated vehicle rental and the drivers’ freight. Mistyped license plates, driver names, invoice numbers, and amounts, plus broken formulas, delayed the audits and made them harder; I built the tool to put an end to that.

Problem, system, and proof

Problem

License plates, drivers, invoices, and amounts typed in by hand, with broken formulas.

System

Tracks fuel deliveries and freight: reads the NF-e XML, calculates vehicle rental and driver pay, and closes the month in PDF or Excel.

Proof

145 tests

120 unit and 25 Firestore rules tests, on GitHub Actions. Checked on 9 Nov 2026.
