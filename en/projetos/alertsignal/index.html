AlertSignal

Permit and license deadlines with automatic alerts.

github.com/alyssom-fernandes/AlertSignalScreens published in the repository
How to run the demoCode

Why it exists

Expiration dates for business permits, AVCB (fire safety certificate), and other licenses of the group’s companies were tracked in spreadsheets, but they didn’t always get opened for a check. He developed the tool to notify the people responsible for each license when its expiration date gets close.

Problem, system, and proof

Problem

Deadlines that depended on someone opening the spreadsheet.

System

Tracks permit and license expirations for multiple companies, with daily email alerts starting 90 days before each deadline and higher urgency at 30 and 7 days.

Proof

62 tests

unittest on GitHub Actions. Checked on 9 Nov 2026.

No hosted demo: it runs on your own computer, with fictitious data, following the README.
