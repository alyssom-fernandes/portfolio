Docke

Documents for multiple companies, with an audit trail.

docke-two.vercel.appScreens from the public demo, with fictitious data
Open the demoCode

Why it exists

The group was going to migrate all its documentation to the cloud. I developed Docke to meet the key points of that migration, shaped to the teams’ routine.

Problem, system, and proof

Problem

Documents from multiple companies to organize in the cloud, with deadlines and legal retention.

System

Electronic document management for multiple companies: search in scanned text (OCR), compliance deadlines, legal retention, and a hash-chained audit trail.

Proof

89 tests

83 backend and 6 end-to-end, on GitHub Actions. Checked on 9 Nov 2026.

Inside

  1. docke-two.vercel.app

    Company dashboard

    Documents, folders, anchored items, and recent uploads for the selected company, with documents by folder.

  2. docke-two.vercel.app

    Folders with OCR

    Each folder lists its documents with type, size, and the OCR mark, which lets you search inside the scanned text.

  3. docke-two.vercel.app

    Audit trail

    Every upload, view, and download is logged in a hash-chained trail.

  4. On a phone too

    The same dashboard, with navigation at the bottom.

Stack

React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, FastAPI, PostgreSQL (RLS), Supabase, Tesseract, pytest, Playwright
