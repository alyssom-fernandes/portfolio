UnifyData

From store purchases to payroll deductions.

Monthly close of the purchases employees make on credit at the group’s stores (gas station, restaurant, hotel), recorded in AutoSystem: it matches them against HR records and generates the payroll deduction spreadsheet.

  1. AutoSystem × HRThe stores’ credit purchase reports are loaded alongside the HR employee records.
  2. Flags what doesn’t matchA purchase with no HR record or no company becomes an open item, with amount, employee ID, and store.
  3. Payroll deductionsGenerates the deduction spreadsheet, one tab per company, plus a CSV and an A4 report.
  4. Web and PythonThe web version, the one in production, matches by employee ID and also works on a phone; the Python version (Streamlit) matches by name, with human review.

Praxis

From request to payment, in a single flow.

Corporate purchasing from request to payment, in 7 stages: side-by-side quotes, approvals, installments, deadlines, and time per stage.

  1. From request to paymentAll 7 stages on a single board, with each column’s total in BRL. Each card shows the company, the amount, and how many days are left until the deadline.
  2. Side-by-side quotesProposals on the same screen, with price, difference from the lowest, delivery time, and terms. The recommended one is flagged.
  3. 6 access rolesEach request shows who needs to act now and the savings compared with the most expensive proposal. The workflow covers requester, buyer, approvers, and finance, each with their own permissions.
  4. Installs as an appA PWA: the same flow on a phone, with the stages as tabs and navigation at the bottom.

DeepChat

One AI answers. Another reviews.

A chat where DeepSeek answers and ChatGPT reviews the answer with a verdict, strengths, what to improve, and, when there’s an error, a revised version. No server: everything runs in the browser, and API keys, if saved, are stored encrypted.

  1. Verdict up frontEvery answer gets a verdict: correct, partially correct, or incorrect.
  2. Revised versionIn the demo, a function that validates a CPF (Brazilian taxpayer ID) comes back partially correct because it accepts 111.111.111-11, and the review provides the corrected version.
  3. No serverQuestions go straight from the browser to the APIs, and API keys, if saved, are stored encrypted. The demo needs no key.
  4. On a phone tooThe same chat and the same review, with a layout built for phones.

AudiStock

From the stock count to the shortage and overage report.

Inventory audits: the team counts on a phone, enters the ERP balance at closing, and gets a shortage and overage report in PDF, Excel, and CSV, with an offline queue for counting without internet.

  1. Audits in progressThe progress of each count, a summary of the latest audit, and the largest shortages and overages relative to the balance.
  2. Counts on a phoneKeyboard, barcode scanner, or camera, with an offline queue for counting without internet.
  3. Shortages and overages against the ERPAt closing, the team enters the ERP balance. The report shows the system quantity, the counted quantity, and the difference for each item, and exports to PDF, Excel, and CSV.
