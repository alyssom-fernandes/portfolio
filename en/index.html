Career

From the file room to payroll and HR

I started at Astcon Assessoria Contábil in February 2021 as a part-time filing clerk, while still in high school. I moved through accounting and on to payroll and HR: full routines for more than 60 client companies, from hiring to eSocial and DCTFWeb filings. I became the person in charge of payroll and HR and, in practice, of the office: I coordinated the team, trained new hires, and handled the most difficult clients. In Domínio (payroll software), I automated payroll calculation and submission to eSocial, and I rolled out Onvio, the firm’s online portal for clients.

Office Assistant, Feb 2021 – Sep 2025

An invitation to manage

A client of the firm recommended me to the owner of Fazenda Novo México, who was looking for someone to take over administrative management. In September 2025, I joined as an administrative assistant to succeed the manager: staff pay, purchasing control, checking invoices and boletos (bank payment slips), and meetings with the irrigation teams. In January 2026, the group moved the farms’ administration to headquarters, and we parted ways. That is where I made the tool that came before all my projects: an hours calculator for staff pay.

Administrative Assistant, Sep 2025 – Jan 2026

The turning point of 2026

A consultant who knew my work recommended me to Grupo Zen, a group of more than 20 companies: fuel stations, a TRR (fuel distributor), hotels, restaurants, a vehicle rental company, real estate, and holding companies. I joined the financial control team to work directly with the controller and began developing in-house systems. I keep the LMC (the mandatory fuel stock ledger) for three fuel stations, check invoices before the group pays, handle purchasing for some of the companies, and take requests directly from the directors.

Financial Control Assistant, Feb 2026 – present

Turning spreadsheets into systems

After five years inside spreadsheets, I know where they break: a file that doesn’t talk to the next one, reconciliation done by hand, a deadline that depends on someone’s memory. That is what I automate today. I have been teaching myself programming since 2022, and I graduated in Systems Analysis and Development in 2026.
