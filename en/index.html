ArtMaker

Deal flyers ready for WhatsApp Status.

Someone asked him for help with the promo graphics for her grocery store. Instead of making the graphics and keeping her dependent on him, he built the tool so she could make them herself.

Problem

Every promo graphic would depend on someone who knew how to put it together.

System

  1. With the store’s brand
  2. 8 ready-made palettes
  3. Ready for Status

The app’s default is Mercado Vitória. Store and deal details go into the editor, next to the flyer preview.

Palettes appear as thumbnails, drawn by the flyer’s own rendering engine.

The flyer comes out at 1080×1920, with Share and Download, and stays saved on the device.

UnifyData

Calculates payroll deductions for employee purchases.

Employees buy from the group’s companies “on a tab”, with each purchase noted on a paper slip, and the slips are recorded in AutoSystem. For the payroll deduction, someone had to pull a report from each company and build the HR spreadsheet in Excel; HR uses Domínio (payroll software).

Problem

One report per company, combined by hand in Excel for HR.

System

  1. AutoSystem and HR side by side
  2. Flags what doesn’t match
  3. Payroll deduction spreadsheet

The stores’ credit purchase reports are loaded alongside the HR employee records.

A purchase with no HR record or no company becomes an open item, with amount, employee ID, and store.

One tab per company, plus CSV and an A4 report.

Proof

17 in the web version and 23 in the Python version, on GitHub Actions. Checked on 9 Nov 2026.

DeepChat

DeepSeek answers and ChatGPT checks the answer.

AI answers aren’t always reliable. He used to carry the same question from one AI to another until both reached a consensus, and being the messenger got tiring.

Problem

Checking an answer meant copying and pasting between two AIs.

System

  1. Verdict up front
  2. Revised version
  3. On a phone too

Every answer gets a verdict: correct, partially correct, or incorrect.

In the demo, a function that validates a CPF (Brazilian taxpayer ID) comes back partially correct because it accepts 111.111.111-11, and the review provides the corrected version.

The same chat and the same review, with a layout built for phones.

Praxis

From request to payment, in a single flow.

Organizing the group’s purchasing: the more the group grew, the harder it was to track and control each request.

Problem

Requests, quotes, and approvals that were hard to track.

System

  1. From request to payment
  2. Side-by-side quotes
  3. Installs as an app

A PWA: the same flow on a phone, with the stages as tabs and navigation at the bottom.

AudiStock

Stock counts on a phone, checked against the ERP.

The people auditing stock at some of the group’s companies reported problems with AutoSystem’s counting. He built AudiStock to work like AutoSystem and integrate with it, solving the operators’ complaints.

Problem

Stock counts drew complaints from the auditors.

System

  1. Audits in progress
  2. Counts on a phone
  3. Shortages and overages vs. the ERP

The progress of each count, a summary of the latest audit, and the largest shortages and overages relative to the balance.

Keyboard, barcode scanner, or camera, with an offline queue for counting without internet.

At closing, the team enters the ERP balance. The report shows the system quantity, the counted quantity, and the difference for each item, and exports to PDF, Excel, and CSV.

Proof

84 unit and database tests and 46 end-to-end, on GitHub Actions. Checked on 9 Nov 2026.

Fuel Mind

Reads fuel invoices (NF-e) and closes the month.

Fuel deliveries were entered in one spreadsheet per fuel station and TRR (fuel distributor), then carried over to another one that calculated vehicle rental and the drivers’ freight. Typing errors caused delays and made audits harder.

Problem

License plates, drivers, invoices, and amounts typed in by hand, with broken formulas.

System

  1. Imports the NF-e
  2. Rental and freight
  3. Closes the month

Reads the XML of the electronic invoice (NF-e) to record each fuel delivery.

Freight month by month, with vehicle rental and driver pay at month-end close.

Monthly close in PDF or Excel.

AlertSignal

Permit and license deadlines with automatic alerts.

Expiration dates for business permits, AVCB (fire safety certificate), and other licenses of the group’s companies sat in spreadsheets that didn’t always get opened. The tool alerts each person in charge when an expiration date gets close.

Problem

Deadlines that depended on someone opening the spreadsheet.

System

  1. Overview
  2. Daily email alerts
  3. Expiration report

Expired, due for renewal, current, and undated, with a chart of the next 12 months.

Each company shows its documents by urgency, with expiration date and time left. Alerts start 90 days before, with higher urgency at 30 and 7.

Filters by status and exports to PDF, Excel, and CSV.

Docke

Documents for multiple companies, with an audit trail.

The group was going to move all its documents to the cloud. He developed Docke around the key points of that move, shaped to the teams’ routine.

Problem

Documents from multiple companies to organize in the cloud, with deadlines and legal retention.

System

  1. Company dashboard
  2. Folders with OCR
  3. Audit trail

Documents, folders, anchored items, and recent uploads for the selected company, with documents by folder.

Each folder lists its documents with type, size, and the OCR mark, which lets you search inside the scanned text.

Every upload, view, and download is logged in a hash-chained trail.
